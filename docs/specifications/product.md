# RoboBuild product specification

Status: product requirements for four LEGO robots, not a claim of completion. The [current coverage ledger](../validation/coverage-baseline.md) records the interactive reconstructions and remaining limitations. Section 18 of the user handoff has **12 acceptance criteria**, mapped verbatim in [testing](testing.md); “18” is a section number, not a count of criteria. All 12 apply independently to each of the four builds.

## Product and completion contract

Build an original premium SvelteKit + TypeScript + Three.js WebGL2 application for learning how four specific LEGO robots are assembled and operate. One shared assembly engine, viewer and timeline consume source-backed per-build data. Geometry, assembly graph, source evidence and simulation data are separate from rendering. Adding a robot primarily adds its manifest, attributable geometry, BOM, steps, connections/joints and behavior configuration, not another viewer implementation.

The user journey is: choose an accurately identified build; inspect its real staged components and counts; assemble individual parts or appropriate mounting subassemblies through source-linked reversible motion; inspect the same resulting hierarchy; then operate a useful post-assembly demonstration. Every visible control must change the advertised state, or be explicitly disabled with an accessible reason. No generic robots, decorative controls, invented steps, simultaneous completed-model reveal, teleport mounting, unrelated simulation models or fake completion badges.

The four-build goal is not reduced to research, this specification or a vertical slice. Finish verified assemblies rather than four superficial approximations. Any prerequisite that prevents a build's completion remains explicit and blocks that build's acceptance. Do not market blocked simulation/playback as available. Historical VEX research is outside the active product and creates no runtime, licensing or delivery dependency.

## Catalog and availability

Display exactly the four LEGO builds with manufacturer, category, model/manual names, edition, scoped documented count, qualitative complexity and its mechanical reason, assembly completion state, implemented/verified coverage and official sources. Complexity is explanatory, not a measured effort/time claim. Selecting a build loads that build's own dataset and resets no other build by accident.

Each catalog entry resolves to its own `RobotModel` and interactive workbench, with an attractive preview generated from actual runtime geometry. Reconstruction and verification limitations remain explicit; partial geometry must show actual n/N source-step coverage rather than an imaginary completed preview. `CatalogEntry` carries identity, model URL, preview URL and coverage, without a status discriminant. The active catalog contains no blocked source-only variant or sources-only dialog.

## Workspace and source workflow

The original dark graphite/slate laboratory workbench is specified in [visual design](visual-design.md). Desktop uses left instructions/sources, central 3D, right inventory/inspection and persistent bottom transport. Panels collapse into accessible drawers/sheets on smaller screens without losing warnings, source access, step state or inventory. No horizontal mobile overflow.

Each active step shows an original concise instruction, current module/subassembly, required parts with quantities, operation verb, mounting relationship, warnings, exact document/page/printed-step/figure reference, and evidence classification. Use actual part previews or independently authored schematics; official page imagery, PDF scans and program artwork remain private unless permission is obtained. Always link the official source. Export metadata is not a claimed publication revision.

Selecting a step seeks its start, pauses playback, selects/highlights relevant actual instances, updates hardware/source text and frames the active operation only if automatic camera is enabled. Scrubbing updates current operation and source reference from sampled time. Source-page browsing is independent: one-based PDF page and printed step are separately labeled; book step numbering may restart. Selecting an explicitly mapped source-figure button seeks the mapped assembly state. Reference-only/warning/preparation pages without a mapped assembly operation do not become invented numbered steps or alter time. Many-to-one/one-to-many page mappings must remain explicit, with a chooser when needed.

## Deterministic assembly and accounting

Every physical/runtime instance has a stable ID, real geometry/material identity, final transform, connection points, hierarchy membership and evidence/confidence. A geometry ID is not a manufacturer part/element number; omit uncertain manufacturer identifiers and show descriptive corrected family names. Prepared devices may contain rigid render subparts without increasing source unit counts. Reused subassemblies retain their internal transforms and are not recounted when mounted. A four-pin callout introduces four instances, not one grouped inventory item.

Scene-facing transforms use right-handed Y-up millimeters, normalized `[x,y,z,w]` quaternions and default `[1,1,1]` scale. Preserve native origins; LDraw's approximate 0.4 mm/LDU and root rotation X = pi are not metrology. Source camera turns are camera presets, never physical part rotations. Each operation records actual participating instances, absolute from/to transforms, dependencies, source and provenance. Insert/slide/rotate/fasten/align/attach/connect/merge-subassemblies have continuous authored paths, not fading in at final positions. Screw placement and fastening can be distinct operations. Parent changes preserve world-space continuity and mechanical connections. Dependencies form a valid DAG; no part is fastened before its supporting structure exists.

A genuine numbered source camera/view-turn step is represented by a meaningful presentation-only `align` operation with empty part/transform/staging arrays, a required camera preset and source evidence. It changes neither physical transforms nor counts and respects manual camera override. Other vacuous empty/no-effect operations are invalid coverage. Wiring and reused-module steps may reference existing instances and add zero new units; a step need not introduce a part to be implemented.

At time zero, show an appealing organized inventory of genuine parts or appropriate prepared units in deterministic staging poses, with correct quantities. During assembly, parts travel from staging/previous poses along source-appropriate paths and align with their mating geometry. At final time the same hierarchy is the completed assembly; no replacement model swap. Presentation staging and path timing are labeled app-authored where appropriate, not manufacturer instructions or certified collision-free tool procedures.

The engine contract coordinated with engineering is `compileAssembly(model)`, `createFrame(compiled)`, `sampleAssembly(compiled, timeMs, out)`. UI owns finite absolute `timeMs`, playback direction/speed/mode. Sampling the same timestamp yields the same part/internal-node transforms, hierarchy, active/installed/connected flags and counts regardless of playback history; the output frame is reused, not newly allocated each animation frame. Connection state supports reversible wiring. Internal rotor/body render nodes preserve actual device motion without creating extra inventory units. Source step actions distinguish assembly/view/wiring/reuse independently of numbered-step status. Renderer-owned camera/selection/inspection state does not change physical assembly samples.

Every installation event has an explicit timestamp derived from its operation, normally the moment a part reaches its installed endpoint; staged/in-flight parts remain pending until that event. Multi-part operations can have distinct per-instance events, so four pins update counts one by one. At the exact event timestamp the part is installed; reversing below it removes it from installed counts. Counts are sampled from instance state, never accumulated `++`/`--` or maintained as percentages. Distinguish installed, active/in-flight and remaining; an installed part undergoing a later fastening operation is not counted a second time. Show robot and samples separately, fasteners where relevant, current subassembly and complete/remaining source steps. Preparation-only events remain separately labeled, not fabricated source steps.

## Transport behavior

All actions have visible controls, accessible names and working disabled states at boundaries. Source-step boundaries are absolute queries over the compiled timeline, not assumptions that each source step has one uniform duration. Source numbering is distinct from fractional animation progress.

| Control | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Play step | Run forward from current time to the next source-step end and stop exactly. At an exact boundary run the next step; at final time do not silently restart. |
| Play all | Run forward from current time to final time, stop there. Distinct from Play step. |
| Pause / Resume | Pause preserves the exact sampled time; resume preserves active direction/speed/play scope and endpoint. No scene jump. A stopped boundary is not silently restarted by Pause. |
| Reverse | Play continuously backward from current time to zero; no negative time or invented reverse instructions. It is an explanatory reversal, not a certified safe disassembly procedure. |
| Previous / Next | Pause first. Previous from within a step seeks that step's start; from an exact boundary seeks the preceding boundary. Next seeks the next boundary. Clamp at endpoints. |
| Timeline scrub | Pointer or keyboard seek to any finite time in range; pause immediately; sample deterministically in either direction and update sources/counts. Native arrow keys make small labeled increments; Home/End seek endpoints. |
| Speed | Offer 0.25×, 0.5×, 1× and 2×; changing speed preserves time and endpoint. Speed affects temporal traversal, not path, placement or count rules. |
| Milestones | Source-backed book/module/integration boundaries; selecting pauses and seeks the named boundary with its official step reference. No invented milestone masquerades as a numbered source step. |

Playback advances from elapsed time, not frame count. Background-tab recovery clamps to intended scope boundaries without overshooting. Loading/model changes stop the previous playback and release its resources. An unavailable timestamp/model state has an explicit error rather than a silent default robot.

## Camera, modes and inspection

Provide orbit, pan and zoom with pointer/touch and keyboard-accessible visible alternatives; Reset view, Fit whole assembly, Fit selected subassembly/selection, Focus active operation and Resume auto camera are distinct actions. Fit uses actual visible bounds, including partial/exploded geometry where relevant, not an empty placeholder. User manipulation immediately disables automatic transitions until explicit Resume auto camera; selecting steps must not fight a user's camera. Reset returns the current authored useful view without secretly resuming automatic following. Author camera presets separately from part transforms.

Studio, Technical, Explode and Inspection use the same geometry and sampled assembly state. Studio has neutral premium PBR treatment, controlled tone mapping/environment lights, soft shadows, antialiasing and bounded adaptive quality. Technical offers clean contrasting edges/silhouettes and selected outlines. Explode meaningfully separates the currently present parts/subassemblies, preserves source time and counts, shows relationships and exactly restores the saved assembly view on exit; offsets are presentation-only. Inspection supports isolate/exit isolate and part/subassembly focus without mutating progress. Switching modes never completes assembly or unlocks simulation.

Ray-pick actual stable instance IDs. Selection synchronizes scene outline, inventory row and inspector; labels and text work when color cannot. Inspector includes corrected family/name, known catalog identity, geometry ID, instance ID, parent/subassembly, relevant operation, installation state, connections/mount points and source/provenance. Inventory search/filter and row selection work for repeated and staged instances. Selecting a not-yet-installed part does not install it. Labels, relationships and mount points can be toggled without changing transforms. Educational overlays cover joint axes, motor/gear rotation and transmission direction, gripping mechanism, sensor operation, structural support and dependency relationships where backed by the data; visibly distinguish inferred explanations from verified geometry/physics. A minimal mode removes clutter while keeping essential warnings and selection feedback.

## Post-assembly demonstration contract

Simulation is enabled only when the **complete supported robot assembly** is at its final installed state, not merely the end of a partial slice. Full required sample preparation is checked before a sample-dependent scenario begins. Exploding, scrubbing or hiding parts cannot bypass this gate. Entering simulation pauses assembly and uses the same actual part/subassembly/joint hierarchy; joint transforms are applied relative to the completed assembly pose. Leaving/resetting simulation restores that pose and zeroes object/command/sensor state deterministically. Reset simulation does not erase source evidence or restart assembly.

Provide fixed/revolute/prismatic joints as needed, correctly located axes/pivots, coupled constraints, source- or measurement-backed safe ranges, motor position/speed commands and visible sensor events. Unknown mechanical limits remain unknown; a conservative app demonstration range is separately labeled, not presented as a manufacturer's stop. No fabricated independent actuator is allowed. Deterministic kinematics are sufficient where they actually implement the requested behavior; add dynamics only if the actual interaction requires it. Object contact/holding/release must be coherent with geometry and joint state, not an unrelated animation or unconditional object teleport. Scripted sensor/object responses and uncalibrated parameters are clearly labeled **RoboBuild simulation extension / qualitative demonstration**, never real robot validation.

|---|---|---|
| Advanced Driving Base | Differential-drive obstacle course with forward/reverse/turn, independent supported unloaded tool-output controls, two color-sensor signals, course/robot reset | Two Large propulsion motors, two Medium output motors, two Color Sensors. No strafing or invented optional tool accessories. Course/contact and floor response are app-authored; wheel geometry/zeros/clearance must be resolved before verification. |
| Robot Hand | One powered coordinate drives actual coupled jaws/linkage through grasp, hold and release of a sample, with opening/closing command and reset | Not independent fingers; no installed Force Sensor or tilt actuator. Measured/CAD pivots, closed-loop solution and bounds required. Sample/grip/contact rules are explicitly qualitative/app-authored; preserve lesson prose/SVG control discrepancy. |

| Package Sorter | Complete sample-object pickup, color-check, transport and drop cycle using actual base yaw and linked fork lift; controls for axes/cycle/reset and sensor event display | A yaw, F fork lift, D Color Sensor; no conveyor or independent gripper. Fork support/contact and linkage must be resolved. Preserve source program-order/speed-unit differences; added generalized sorting rules are an extension, not inferred official arbitrary multi-bin behavior. |

Show the active DOF/command, units, configured demonstration bounds and evidence/extension label beside each control. No payload, grip force, safe travel, sensor accuracy, tipping or calibrated physics claim without measured validation. Missing numerical evidence is a prerequisite to completing the relevant scenario, not permission to silently omit it or supply fake defaults.

## Keyboard and accessibility

A persistent Keyboard help button exposes shortcuts and gesture alternatives. Default workspace shortcuts: `[`/`]` previous/next; Space pause/resume (when idle, Play step); `R` Reset view; `F` Fit whole; `X` Explode; `L` Labels; `T` Technical; `I` Inspection; `?` Help; Escape exits a transient dialog/drawer/isolation in the currently focused context. Keep visible buttons for every action, including Play all and Reverse. Announce shortcut changes if bindings differ on a platform.

Do not capture shortcuts while focus is in input, textarea, select, editable content, slider or an open dialog with its own key contract, nor during IME composition or modified system/browser shortcuts. Native buttons keep native activation semantics; Space on a focused button does not also toggle playback. Keyboard handlers apply only to their active workspace/context, never globally to an inactive model. Sliders keep standard arrow/Home/End behavior. Modal help/drawers trap focus only if actually modal, close on Escape and restore focus to the invoking control. No keyboard trap in the canvas.

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, meaningful visible focus, appropriate labels/status announcements and a DOM equivalent for critical canvas information. Respect reduced motion with immediate step/camera transitions and explicit opt-in to moving demonstrations. Keep Pause accessible at all times. Touch targets, no horizontal overflow at 320 px/200% zoom, responsive drawers, landscape and narrow viewport use are acceptance requirements, not inferred from desktop appearance.

## Fidelity, delivery and claims

Expose separate source identity/order, component identity, geometry, placement, kinematics and simulation confidence. A numeric confidence field is not a calibrated probability; retain its evidence category and caveat. Use source-verified, CAD-derived, measured, diagram-derived, inferred and presentation-only labels as appropriate. Unknown manufacturer IDs, matrices and dimensions stay unknown. Never relabel an approximation as exact.

Preserve root/dependency attribution and mixed-license histories through conversion; show accessible source/license documentation. Downloaded manuals, native private CAD, page imagery and benchmark screenshots stay private. Source linking is not a conversion license; every distributed asset requires established applicable rights.

Delivery proceeds through research, specifications, a genuine-geometry verified Advanced Driving Base subassembly, full Driving Base plus demonstration, remaining complete builds/demonstrations, visual refinement and comprehensive validation. Each phase is intermediate, not a replacement for the complete deliverable. Final handoff includes the running app, all four LEGO configurations, source-backed manifests/component library, simulations, permanent tests, user/architecture documentation, research provenance, validation reports and known limitations. Report substantial unimplemented/unverified steps or incomplete required demonstrations as incomplete. Actual evidence requirements are in [testing.md](testing.md); current implementation and unresolved requirements are in the [coverage ledger](../validation/coverage-baseline.md).
