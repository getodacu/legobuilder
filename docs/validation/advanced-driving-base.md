# Advanced Driving Base — full-source authoring, not full-fit verification

The five official [2019 LEGO Education building-instruction books](https://education.lego.com/en-us/product-resources/spike-prime/downloads/building-instructions/) document 38 + 24 + 24 + 31 + 19 = **136 numbered steps**. The authored manifest now represents all 136 steps and 73 + 39 + 39 + 68 + 15 = **234 unique prepared source-callout units**. Book 5 reuses four physically animated module roots and introduces only its own fifteen units; the hub with installed battery is **one** prepared unit, the wheel includes its tyre, and motor rotors and captive cables add **zero** units. The legacy first-seven identity and datum remain unchanged. Implemented source coverage is **not** proof of 136 correct fits.

### Demonstration provenance and limits

Four bindings are to actual bodies/rotors and child subassemblies: left `A`, right `E` large powered wheels; front `D` and rear `C` medium independently rotating gear outputs. Color sensor B/F source mapping follows crossed Book5 page5 wires. Motor angular rates and color events are explicit app-authored educational inputs. Wheel nominal radius is 44mm from source family `88mm`, actual loaded mesh outer radius43.9058mm; neither substitutes a physical manufacturer tolerance. Differential axle midpoint is the **actual completed pair of wheel centers**, not the h1 Front datum. Real non-BOM `robobuild-course-marker` GLBs are authored original props with source SHA and outside the source/manual robot counts; no imaginary accessory tools or manufacturer-licensed course parts. No collision dynamics, optical physics, motor polarity calibration or cable bending precision is asserted.

The catalog presents **136 instructions authored**, never **136 independently verified**. Strict source compilation and unit checks ran, and actual WebGL rendered the first seven, an in-flight rear approach, finished structure, exploded/technical views and a completed-model course. Left/right wheels and independent source C/D motor outputs use the very same assembled hierarchy. The four physical rear-to-side links **still fail independent registration** (64 mm free rear-hole span versus 96 mm pin span), and other source/collision fits remain unchecked. Hand, Quality Check and Package Sorter are now available as independently authored models, **not** fit-approved builds; VEX app CAD conversion remains permission-blocked. Full acceptance requires measuring source views and clearance of all five books, repairing mismatches, checking cable travel and calibrating safe travel.
