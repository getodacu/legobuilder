# Flatpack interaction benchmark

Observed 2026-10-20 in a real Chromium browser at [Flat Pack, Step by Step](https://sael.net/flatpack/) and its [LACK state](https://sael.net/flatpack/#lack). This is an interaction/visual benchmark, **not** a source-code, geometry, performance, accessibility-conformance, or licensing audit. RoboBuild requirements below are independently proposed equivalents, not claims about implemented RoboBuild features.

## Method and evidence

The live application, its rendered manual imagery, visible help, accessible control names and rendered DOM text were inspected. Actions included actual pointer clicks/drags/wheel input and keyboard input. Desktop inspection used a 1440 × 1000 viewport; narrow-screen inspection used 390 × 844 with mobile/touch emulation. No source code, meshes, textures, manual files, or proprietary implementation assets were copied. Screenshots of the rendered application/manual are **private development evidence**, stored only in `.research-cache/flatpack/`, not application/public assets or redistribution permission.

The first browser session collided with other research tabs because named tabs attached to the same Chrome target. Those interrupted sequences are not used to establish timing or playback boundaries. Remaining interactions were exercised in a separately launched headless Chromium instance on a dedicated debugging port/profile. An execution lasting over the browser helper's default 30 seconds timed out; the resulting state was subsequently observed rather than assuming a successful return. This is why no load-time/FPS/animation-duration benchmark is claimed.

## Playback results: tested behavior versus help

The help says “press play to watch one step”; the dedicated camera button says “Play the whole build, with the manual's camera angles (C).” Both were exercised, but **do not assume a confirmed standalone one-step Play mode from this session**:

- **Next-step control:** from BILLY kit state, after settling, timeline was `1.00` and Play tooltip was `Play the build (space)`. This establishes a one-step advance that stops.
- **Play/Space in cinematic context:** BILLY advanced from the step-1 boundary beyond step 2; LACK advanced from 0 to `1.91` and still displayed `Pause (space)`. The C control remained `aria-pressed=true`. This establishes continuous playback in the observed context, not the advertised one-step stopping behavior.
- **Whole playback:** C was invoked for LACK from kit state, and the final state was subsequently observed at `3.00`, `4/4` hardware and `5/5` panels. The concluding step explicitly says the manual leaves turning it over to the user. The intermediate whole-playback call timed out; final state, not that call's successful completion, is the evidence.
- **Backward navigation:** Previous/bracket reversed the step state; no dedicated continuously playing reverse mode was present in the observed controls.

RoboBuild should remove this ambiguity with distinct **Play step**, **Play all**, **Pause**, **Previous** and **Next** actions, and tests of their actual boundaries. An exploded overview should be an inspection state, not silently overwrite saved assembly progress.

## Exactness and rights qualifications in Flatpack's help

Source: the live site's **How it works** dialog ([application](https://sael.net/flatpack/), E1; same BILLY-centered help was shown while LACK was selected).

- It identifies BILLY as 80 × 28 × 202 cm and manual `AA-1823127-9`, and claims the same parts, counts, order and hardware numbers as the manual, viewed from **roughly** each drawing's angle. These are **the site's claims**, not validation of its model by this research.
- Its exactness statement says: “panel thickness, hole positions and the shelf heights are read off the drawings, so they are close, not measured.” Published overall bookcase dimensions are not a claim that every 3D feature has measured CAD accuracy.
- Animation is much faster than real assembly; the manual asks for two people for lifting/turning. Wall fixing is a critical warning; wall screws/plugs depend on the wall and are not included.
- LACK is identified with manual `AA-2606170-1`. Help explicitly says the manual ends once the legs are attached and the 3D adds turning it over as a third step. This is an excellent example of an inference that must **not** be relabeled as an official manual step.
- The help credits manual copyright to Inter IKEA Systems B.V. and says the experience is not made or endorsed by IKEA. The reader also credits its original manual and links to the original PDF. That site's reproduction statement is **not** a license for RoboBuild to redistribute it or any robot manual.

RoboBuild's own fidelity statement must distinguish **source-verified identity/count/order**, **verified dimensions**, **diagram-derived approximations**, and **presentation-only animation**. A missing measurement, instruction page or inventory stays unknown; plausible geometry is not evidence.

## Explicitly unavailable or not established

No proprietary implementation was inspected. No claims are made about Flatpack's framework, renderer internals, shaders, asset provenance, exact CAD dimensions, licensing permission, persistence, offline support, safe physical simulation, continuous reverse playback, measurement tools, part search/isolation, speech output, WCAG conformance, physical touch usability, FPS or network responsiveness. These were either absent from observed controls or outside this interaction study. Private screenshots must not become public RoboBuild artwork, examples or manual assets.
