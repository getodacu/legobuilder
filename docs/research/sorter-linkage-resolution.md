# Sorter linkage: bearing ownership, spatial closure and the remaining contact equation

## Result and scope

The canonical model has a **real carrier bearing**, not a second keyed motor output or a planar four-bar. Its carrier, asymmetric shaft stack, white F-bearing cage, forward fork adapters, upright panels, yellow/direct-black mast, fixed motor rails and actual pedestal/sample receiving rows now have mutually consistent native-feature registrations. Both genuine40mm rods close at the nominal reference pose without moving a socket or stretching a part. Inspectable material frames and six new behavioral regressions preserve the distinctions. This is nominal source/CAD reconstruction, **not yet a proved contact-sequenced sorting cycle**.

**The complete sorter is not resolved.** No continuous motor-to-carrier/fork branch, source-sample capture or delivery is claimed. The source supports an underactuated, contact-sequenced gripper hypothesis; two fixed-length ball links alone do not uniquely determine carrier pitch plus two fork swivels. The exact missing item is a source-registered **active stop/contact equation**, not a gear ratio or an arbitrary rod length. See the equations and unresolved registration below. Existing pickup-disabled scenarios and the explicit full-cycle skip remain in place.

Owned files: `src/lib/models/package-sorter.ts`, `tests/unit/package-sorter.test.ts`, this report. No shared solver, research inventory, catalog, generated manifest or global validation report is changed here. All historical part IDs, 158 robot + 8 sample instances, 80 numbered instructions and canonical document identities remain unchanged.

## Predictions that distinguish the topology

1. **Misassigned bearing frame.** If B2pp21–22 is a round 7L rear-end bearing, its nativeY axis must be parallel to F's transverse axis, while it need not rotate rigidly with the keyed shaft. The old model fails the axis test even though the hole centre lies on F. This is the defect fixed here.
2. **Spatial, contact-dependent gripper.** If B2p49's 18651 goes through a round carrier hole and keys the white block, the fork/white rigid group can swivel about the carrier-normal axis. With a free carrier pitch and two spherical rods, unloaded motion needs an additional active physical condition. The film should close/support the package before the large carrier rise and lower/release on reversal. The inspected film is consistent with that sequence, but does not identify a unique nominal contact surface or prove a particular quasi-static selection law.
3. **Wrong panel receiving row.** If B2pp50–52 mounts the prepared bracket to the panel's ten front holes, the bracket pin/axle axes must align with **nativeX** panel holes, not the five-hole nativeY row used by the terminal towball. Merely rotating the current rod socket into place cannot satisfy both independent receiving features.

These predictions distinguish real topology from moving a convenient marker or choosing an artificial angular multiplier.

## Native feature equations and solver implications

Let `H` be the common F/7L bearing line, `e_x` its unit direction, `theta` the source F angle and `phi` carrier pitch. Define the carrier rigid transform

`C(phi) = T(H) Rx(phi) T(-H) C0`.

Each fork has carrier-local pivot `P_i`, unit axis `n_i` normal to the carrier bearing plane and swivel `psi_i`. Its rigid transform is

`J_i(phi,psi_i) = C(phi) T(P_i) R(n_i,psi_i) T(-P_i) J_i0`.

`P_i`, `n_i` and `J_i0` must come from the independently fitted18651/32184/32271 receiving passages. They are **not** free fit parameters to eliminate rod residuals. With crank sphere `a_i` and panel sphere `b_i` transcribed from their native6628 centres,

`A_i(theta) = T(H) Rx(theta) T(-H) A_i0 a_i`,

`B_i(phi,psi_i) = J_i(phi,psi_i) b_i`,

`f_i(theta,phi,psi_i) = ||B_i - A_i||² - 40² = 0` in mm.

The spherical rod is then oriented from one real socket to the other, retaining scale1 and40mm span; socket articulation limits would be additional inequalities, not a made-up rod pitch ratio. Both rod endpoints must independently match their actual ball centres.

For a prescribed `theta`, two scalar equations act on three variables `(phi,psi_left,psi_right)`. Their Jacobian is 2×3 and rank is at most2; therefore length closure alone cannot prove a unique one-dimensional driven branch. Imposing `psi_left = -psi_right` adds a third equation but **would be forced symmetry**, not a demonstrated material constraint. A planar closed-loop solver cannot cure the missing equation by choosing a convenient branch.

A contact-based continuation could become well-defined only after independently registering its active material features. For signed separation `g_k(q)`, nonpenetration is `g_k(q) >= 0`; an active stop/contact adds `g_k(q)=0`. Candidate modes are an unloaded lower carrier stop, contact with the same two source gear feet, and release from those supports. The active-set transition must be based on those real surfaces, not a scripted capture time. A quasi-static selection would additionally need a stated load/equilibrium model; a dynamic solution would need masses/contact/friction parameters not supplied by the film. Neither is implemented here.

**No shared solver API is requested yet:** handing core only two rod lengths and three passive angles would force it to invent the missing condition. The exact needed third equation is a registered `g_stop(phi)=0` or source-sample `g_contact(phi,psi_left,psi_right,X_sample)=0`, together with a defensible active-set transition. Nominal CAD geometry is sufficient to define that equation; unknown real tolerances are not the blocker.

The reconstructed nominal geometry also gives a useful **conditional** free-space diagnostic, independently of model execution. If the carrier were held at `phi=0`, the right rod equation in LDU reduces to

`(60 - 60 cos(psi))² + (-20 + 20 cos(theta))² + (100 + 60 sin(psi) + 20 sin(theta))² = 100²`.

It follows directly from the carrier swivel at `(c+120,-290,180)`, terminal sphere at `(c+60,-330,180)` and **inward** input sphere at `(c+60,-330,80)`, with `c=-20`. Each left/right rod can be solved independently; the mirrored values are a property of this nominal fixture, not an added symmetry constraint. A standalone Python calculation produced:

This is a diagnostic of the actual native link dimensions, **not a new animation or a demonstrated operating branch**: holding `phi=0` itself requires a real stop, and `theta` still needs its source encoder phase/sign registration. In particular these conditional values do not explain the source F25→F235 lift. Treating them as the complete sorter would simply hide the missing physical equation.

There is a second useful invariant, not a claimed transmission ratio: with a genuinely held fixed swivel, rod length depends on `phi-theta`, since both its input and carrier-bound endpoint rotate around the same F line. On one continuous fixed-swivel branch this gives `delta(phi)=delta(theta)`. Source F25→F235 is a negative unwrapped input change whereas the film carrier rises; therefore the API-angle→physical-crank sign must be registered rather than assuming the old positive sceneX axis. Neither a convenient sign/phase nor a gravity/friction selection law is silently enabled.

## Exact remaining registration and acceptance boundary

The following are unresolved source registration, not tolerance certification:

- The new native-feature carrier/fork registration and nominal rotor/gear stack still require the parent's integrated verification; exact historical molds and non-feature unintended intersections are not certified here.
- The source encoder phase/sign must be registered jointly with carrier pitch and fork swivel zeroes. Nominal reference rod closure is now exact, but no active-contact driven branch is enabled.
- Yellow/mast, fixed rails, cage, pedestal and full three-hole package row are now nominally registered; their loaded passive state/unintended intersections still need integrated source/consumer comparison.
- The11L/shell material candidate is not proven to be the active carrier stop. Its gap equation cannot replace full nonpenetration or package contact.
- Material grip/support facets and package profiles are named, but finite3D contacts, A/F phase/sign and a useful route remain unproved. An A-yaw centre offset alone is neither contact proof nor impossibility proof: finite packages/caps and independent swivels matter. No sample is shifted to force a route. Capture/ejection/reset remain disabled rather than fabricating held transport or a ballistic throw.

Consequently continuous closure over the required source interval `F ∈ [−125°,25°]` is **not demonstrated**, and useful sorting remains blocked. The endpoint/root-bearing correction is real and independently testable, but it is not promoted to whole-mechanism acceptance. Preserving the existing true source objects without false capture is intentional, not a claim that the physical task has been completed.
