# Advanced Driving Base — source-locked assembly research

Research date: 2026-10-20. This is a research deliverable, not a claim that the full robot has been implemented or simulated.

## Sources and scope

- [Official lesson](https://education.lego.com/en-us/lessons/prime-competition-ready/assembling-an-advanced-driving-base/) identifies four independently built modules, their final assembly, cable-management checks, **two Large Motors for propulsion, two Medium Motors for tools, and two Color Sensors**. It requires SPIKE Prime plus its Expansion Set.
- [Official building-instructions index](https://education.lego.com/en-us/product-resources/spike-prime/downloads/building-instructions/) names Front Part, Right Side, Left Side, Rear Part, and Assembly. Its subsequent **Tools** section is separate. None of the five inspected manuals integrates Dozer Blade, Lift Arm, Crates, or Mission Modules; these are excluded.
- All five PDFs were downloaded from both official pages and compared byte-for-byte: **each lesson-linked file is identical to its differently addressed download-index counterpart**. Asset-URL timestamp-looking strings are therefore not evidence of changed content.
- All **140 PDF pages**, including all **136 numbered steps**, were inspected as rendered imagery. Extracted text alone contains counts and step numbers, not identities or placements. Full per-page text, exact step/page links, new-parts callouts, and editorial observations are in [structured evidence](data/advanced-driving-base.json).

Downloaded manuals and rendered contact sheets/details are private development artifacts under `.research-cache/advanced-driving-base/`. They are **not** public/static assets. No redistribution license for the manuals or their images was established.

## Quantity evidence and identifier discipline

Visible new-parts callouts were transcribed at introduction, not counted again when a completed module is attached. In particular, the four complete modules shown in assembly pp1,4–13 do **not** add their parts a second time. Integral motor/sensor cables are not separately counted.

The resulting **callout-unit totals** are Front73, Right39, Left39, Rear68, and final Assembly15: **234 introduced units**, with the hub treated as one assembled hub **including its installed battery**, not a claim of234 individually molded pieces. This is the sum of the checked step callouts, **not** the SPIKE set's advertised element count. An unresolved part identifier does not change its directly visible callout quantity. Per-family totals and all contributing steps are in JSON.

For names, colors and candidate official **element IDs**, compare the [official2021 Expansion Element Overview](https://assets.education.lego.com/v3/assets/blt293eea581807678a/blt3e340817c93629a0/66016a8d9497241adef62cf9/le_spike_prime_expansion_element_overview_45681.pdf?locale=en-us), pp1–2. Its pictured set inventory is **not** used as this build's inventory. The [official2019 overview](https://assets.education.lego.com/v3/assets/blt293eea581807678a/blt28cad37f1f002fd3/5f8801b982eaa522ca601c89/le_spike_prime_element_overview.pdf), p1 core/p2 expansion, establishes contemporary visual families but does not print element IDs. A2021 mold ID must not silently become a verified2019 mold ID.

[Official product information](https://education.lego.com/en-us/teacher-resources/lego-education-spike-prime/support-technical-info/lego-education-spike-prime-support-technical-info-product-info/) separately identifies product SKUs45601 hub,45602 large motor,45603 medium motor,45605 color sensor. **A product SKU is not an element ID or LDraw filename.** Unknown element IDs remain JSON null.

The authoritative contextual [part crosswalk](lego-part-crosswalk.md) and [machine-readable crosswalk](data/lego-part-crosswalk.json) select source geometry/material by **build and stable local key**, not by a key globally. Its `sourceCorrections` records **`black-angle4x4` as light bluish gray `63869.dat`, not a black bent beam** (books2/3 p18; old element `6278125` rejected), and the rear p11/p18/p28 corrections below. The `63869` and other LDraw basenames are geometry IDs, not certified exact2019 molds or manufacturer element IDs. All136 step references and234 physical callout units remain; the mistaken p18 family is removed rather than retained as an alias.

## Kinematics and simulation feasibility

**Verified mechanism topology:** independent left/right wheel outputs are pinned directly to large motors, with outside bearing/support structures; two separate medium motors expose upward gear outputs. There is no visible intervening meshing gear train between a large motor and its propulsion wheel. The two grey28-tooth discs have no mating external gears or integrated tool in these manuals. Two passive ball-in-cup supports and two downward color sensors sit at the front.

**[INFERENCE]** There are four actuated rotational outputs (two propulsion, two tool interfaces). The wheel-to-motor connection implies1:1 output rotation, not an independently geared drive. Planar differential-drive kinematics and free tool-output rotations are feasible; caster balls are passive rolling contacts. Two ground-facing color samples are feasible only after metric sensor registration and floor materials are specified.

**Not verified / do not invent:** wheel-center separation, loaded rolling radius, axle-world transforms, actual mass/inertia/center of mass, motor load/supply curves, floor friction/slip, sensor footprint and height, cable collision behavior, or example-program fidelity.88x14mm is the official catalogue wheel designation, **not a measurement of loaded rolling radius**. Obstacle navigation is an official lesson extension, not evidence that any particular simulation implementation is validated.
