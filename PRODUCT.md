# RoboBuild

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Young builders following a physical robotics build, often on a shared or supervised device. Workshop tools support instructors and builders who want to inspect the assembly in more detail.

## Product Purpose

Help builders identify the pieces, see where they go in 3D, and advance one instruction at a time while using the official manual for the physical build.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Four model guides include source-linked instructions, a parts inventory, and movement demonstrations. Robot Hand is the shortest guide.
- Progress is kept per model in the current browser. The guide can be read and navigated if 3D is unavailable.
- Use attributed geometry and material references. Keep physical-fit warnings, safety instructions, source links, and recorded simulation limitations visible in their relevant workflows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support touch, mouse, keyboard, and reduced motion. Keep step controls and instructions reachable on phones and at browser zoom.
